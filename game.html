Earlier or later?

Is the next look from a later or an earlier decade? Three lives. Reach a streak of eight to win.

How to play

  • The left look is the current one. Guess if the right look is from a later or an earlier decade.
  • Equal decades count as a right guess.
  • A wrong guess costs one life and resets the streak.
  • Keyboard: Arrow Up is Later, Arrow Down is Earlier.
