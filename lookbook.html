Style notes

How to wear it today

You do not need a costume. Take one idea from a wardrobe and mix it with what you already own.

Swap the cloche for a beret and keep one structured piece, like a nipped jacket. Let everything else stay simple.

Pair a colour-block mini with flat boots, or high-waist flares with a plain tucked tee. One loud piece is enough.

Mix a sharp blazer with relaxed jeans. The 80s and the 90s work best when they share one outfit.

Wear one ornamented coat over simple neutrals and let the pattern lead. Gold accents are enough.
